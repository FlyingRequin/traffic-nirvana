Traffic Nirvana — Privacy Policy & Support

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Traffic Nirvana is an offline-capable urban traffic simulation puzzle game for iOS, iPadOS, and Web. We respect your privacy and designed Traffic Nirvana so that it collects no personal data.

2. Data Collection & Storage

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics.
  • No Third-Party Tracking or Ads: Traffic Nirvana contains no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or cross-app tracking technologies.
  • On-Device Game Progress Only: Your high scores, audio/theme preferences, and active city session state are stored strictly locally on your device using standard browser/WebView local storage and iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

3. Postcard Image Sharing

When you use the optional City Postcard feature to save or share a snapshot of your city, the image is generated locally on your device and handed directly to the iOS system Share Sheet or your browser download manager at your request.

4. Children’s Privacy

Traffic Nirvana does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Support & Contact

If you have questions, feedback, or need support with Traffic Nirvana, please visit our project repository at github.com/FlyingRequin/traffic-nirvana.
